# Privacy

ItoCanvas is offline-first. Version 1 does not create an account, display advertising, collect analytics, or transmit option inputs, strategies, or saved analyses to a remote service.

Saved workspace data and exported files remain on the Mac in locations chosen by the user or in the app's local preferences. Removing the app and its local preferences removes app-managed data.

ItoCanvas does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Questions or future privacy changes should be documented in this repository before release.
